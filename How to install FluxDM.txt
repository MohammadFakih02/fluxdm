FluxDM - how to install
=======================

FluxDM is a download manager for Windows 10/11: fast multi-connection downloads that survive restarts,
videos from YouTube and 1000+ sites, and a built-in torrent client.


1. INSTALL
----------
Run FluxDM-Setup.exe (the file name includes the version number).

Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC". That's because the app isn't signed with a paid
certificate, not because something is wrong. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

The setup installs for your Windows account only (no admin password needed) and adds FluxDM to the
Start Menu. It also opens .torrent files with FluxDM.


2. FIRST START
--------------
- The first time you download a torrent, FluxDM asks to be allowed through the Windows firewall.
  Say yes: without it, torrents find far fewer peers and download slowly.
- The first time you download a video, FluxDM fetches two free helper tools (yt-dlp and ffmpeg) by
  itself. That takes a minute once.


3. BROWSER EXTENSION (catches downloads from your browser)
----------------------------------------------------------
In FluxDM, click Settings (top bar), then "Browser extension" on the left. You'll see a button for
each browser on your PC.

Firefox (also Waterfox, LibreWolf, Floorp, Zen):
  Click "Add to Mozilla Firefox" (or your browser), then "Add" in its prompt. Done.

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi:
  Click "Add to Google Chrome" (or your browser). Its extensions page opens together with a folder.
    1. Turn on "Developer mode" (a switch at the top right of that page; in Edge it's on the left).
    2. Click "Load unpacked".
    3. Pick the "BrowserExtension" folder that just opened.
  Leave that folder where it is; the browser loads the extension from it. If Chrome ever warns about
  "developer mode extensions", close the warning with the X. Don't click "Disable".

Check that it works: click the FluxDM icon in the browser's toolbar. It should say "connected" while
FluxDM is running. (If you don't see the icon, look under the puzzle-piece button and pin it.)

When FluxDM isn't running, your browser downloads things normally.


4. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------
- Copying a download link opens FluxDM's Add window automatically. You can turn that off in Settings.
- Closing the window keeps FluxDM in the tray (next to the clock). To quit, right-click the tray icon > Exit.
- To update: run a newer setup over the old one. Your downloads and settings are kept.
- To uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > FluxDM > Uninstall.
  Your download list stays in %APPDATA%\FluxDM in case you reinstall; delete that folder to remove it.
